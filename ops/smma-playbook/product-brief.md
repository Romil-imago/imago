# Product Brief — Imago Growth OS

**Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** Initial strategy; validate with customers

## Vision
Build an international, AI-assisted growth operations platform that lets Imago deliver measurable demand generation for service businesses through repeatable acquisition, conversion, follow-up and reporting systems.

## Initial beachhead
US home-improvement contractors, starting with roofing and kitchen & bath. This is a launch wedge, not a permanent geographic limitation.

## Ideal customer
Owner-led or professionally managed local/regional contractor with a defined service area, clear job types, fulfilment capacity, credible offer and willingness to share baseline conversion data.

## Offer hypothesis
Managed growth system combining landing pages, paid social, lead capture, qualification, consent-aware CRM workflows, appointment conversion and performance reporting. Media spend is separate from service fees and requires client authorization.

## Differentiation
Imago sells an accountable operating system, not cheap-lead promises. Optimisation follows lead quality, contact rate, appointment rate, show rate, close rate and gross profit—not impressions alone.

## North-star metric
Qualified opportunities accepted by the client per active client per month, with appointment and closed-won outcomes included when verified source data is available.

## Business model hypothesis
Setup/onboarding fee plus recurring management fee, with optional creative, funnel and CRM implementation. Do not hard-code pricing before validating willingness to pay and delivery economics.

## Operating principles
1. Consent and privacy are product requirements.
2. No agent launches ads, sends outbound messages, signs contracts or moves money without explicit authorized controls.
3. AI-generated copy and decisions are attributable, reviewable and reversible.
4. Every metric declares its source and whether it is observed, estimated or illustrative.
5. Start narrow; automate repeated, proven work.

## Validation
Interview 10 target contractors; review 3–5 actual lead-to-sale journeys; test one landing page and offer with a small client-approved cap; measure lead-to-contact and contact-to-appointment before scaling.

## Main risks
Offer/market fit, acquisition cost, fulfilment capacity, speed-to-lead, local advertising and messaging regulation, data protection and agency delivery margin.
